Enough Lite demo: narration script (voice: OpenAI gpt-4o-mini-tts, 'nova')

[00 title] Can we retire at sixty? It is the question every household asks. Enough is an AI retirement planner that gives you an honest answer.

[01 how] A team of AI advisers checks your household, one part each. The numbers come from calculations, not from the AI. Let me fold this away and begin.

[02 type] Here is a couple in their fifties. Two earners, about five hundred thousand dollars saved, a mortgage, and a son heading to college. They want to retire at sixty. I tell it in plain words, just as I would tell a friend.

[03 fills] The panel on the right fills in at once. Here is the family picture, and it stays in view. Anything I did not say is a guess, and it is tagged estimate, so I always see what was assumed.

[04 chips] The chat asks short follow-up questions. When its guess is close, I answer with one tap.

[05 fix] When a figure is wrong, I fix it with the pen. Their spending now is nearer one hundred thousand a year, so I change it here.

[06 build] The chat says to check the figures. Then I choose Build my plan, and confirm.

[07 team] Now the advisers work. Each one lights up as it checks its part, and the lines show who passes notes to whom. You can read every hand-off in Team talk. Then the lead adviser writes the plan.

[08 honest] Here is the honest answer. This couple is short about seventeen thousand dollars a year, and the banner says so in plain red. Below it are needs, wants and wishes: what must be paid, what you can count on, and what you hope for.

[09 analysis] The analysis says how sure the plan is, and what could go wrong. If prices rise fast for ten years, savings could run out at sixty five, and they would cut about nine thousand dollars a year. It also shows what each choice is worth.

[10 whatif] So what helps? I ask, what if we retire at sixty four. The advisers run the plan again.

[11 tab] Each What If opens as its own tab, built on the baseline. Here the gap closes, and every change is marked against the baseline.

[12 second] I can add another. Here is an idea from Worth trying.

[13 compare] Compare puts the baseline and both What Ifs side by side. One click on the Baseline tab takes me back.

[14 rule] I can also ask a rule question, like when I can sign up for Medicare. The answer comes with its source.

[15 stock] But ask which stock to buy, and it politely declines. Enough plans. It does not pick stocks.

[16 cost] Under Team talk, token costs show what the plan used: a fraction of a cent. One click shows each adviser's share. And there is a dark mode, for late-night planning.

[17 end] Enough. Can you retire when you want? This is not financial advice.
