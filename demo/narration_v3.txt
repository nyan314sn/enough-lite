Enough Lite demo: narration script (voice: OpenAI gpt-4o-mini-tts, 'nova')

[00 title] Can we retire at sixty? Every household asks. Enough is an AI retirement planner that gives an honest answer.

[01 intro] This is Enough. I tell it about a couple in plain words, the way I would tell a friend. They are in their fifties.

[02 send] The moment I send it, the guide folds into one line. The panel on the right fills in. Anything I did not say is a typical figure, marked estimate.

[03 edit] Every figure in the panel can be changed right there. I press the pencil and pick from the list.

[04 early] I do not have to answer every question. I press Build my plan, and it asks me one thing first: when to retire. I pick sixty.

[05 check] Then Enough asks me to check its guesses. The rows marked estimate are what I left out. They look fair, so I confirm.

[06 team] Now the advisers get to work, one part each. Then the lead adviser writes the plan.

[07 answer] Here is the answer, in one glance. The verdict says not yet. The ring shows how ready they are: seventy out of one hundred. And they are short twenty thousand nine hundred dollars a year.

[08 ladder] Below is the money ladder. Needs are safe, wants are sure, wishes are open. Needs are paid in ninety-seven of one hundred market tests. The couple can count on forty-nine thousand one hundred dollars a year. The wishes are the rest of the goal.

[09 moves] Next come the moves. It is a plain list, and the first one says it closes the gap.

[10 whatif] To test a move, I press What if. The plan greys out, and the What If section lights up. I pick the first idea, the best move.

[11 run] Now the same advisers run the plan again, with that one change.

[12 tab] The result opens as its own tab. The change is to spend forty-nine thousand dollars a year in retirement. Wishes are covered now, and the page says level up. Readiness rises thirty points, to one hundred. That is a trade, and the choice is theirs.

[13 compare] Compare puts them side by side. Before, they were short twenty thousand nine hundred dollars a year, and the goal lasted in six of one hundred tests. After, there is no gap, and it lasts in ninety.

[14 rule] I can also ask a rule question, like when I can sign up for Medicare. The answer comes with its source.

[15 stock] Ask which stock to buy, and Enough politely declines. It plans. It does not pick stocks.

[16 cost] Every plan shows its token costs. This one used well under a cent.

[17 end] Enough. Can you retire when you want? This is not financial advice.
