A partner with little pay of their own gets half of the other's benefit at 67, less if started sooner.
After the first death (at the shorter life expectancy), Social Security falls to the larger of your two benefits, about $67,800 a year, and spending falls to 75% of what the two of you spend.
If you retire before Medicare starts at 65, health cover costs about $10,000 a person a year until then (an assumption). Other health costs follow CMS figures by age.
Tax uses the 2027 federal brackets, the standard deduction and your state's rates. While you work, 401(k) saving comes off taxable pay and the child tax credit applies. In retirement the plan counts 85% of Social Security as taxable, the most the law allows, so the tax may be a little high. Your state taxes part of it too.
