# Product rules

These are the rules the user set for Enough Lite, gathered from the user's instructions. Every rule must be checked by an automated test, an audit check, or both. The coverage table at the end shows which rules have a check and which do not.

## Inputs
- **R1. Propose, then approve.** A skipped, "not sure", "estimate it" or "I don't know" input gets a proposed typical figure. The figure appears in the panel, marked as an estimate, and the reply names it. Once the person approves it, it becomes their own figure. Nothing is filled in silently.
- **R2. Keep stated inputs.** Never change a figure the person gave, unless they confirm a change. When a goal fails, say plainly that it doesn't work on their numbers and offer starting points. Never move their age, and never suggest retiring past 70.
- **R3. One place to type per page.** No page has two places where the person types inputs.
- **R4. Comprehensive inputs.** Cover costs now and future costs (children, college, health before Medicare, home, debts), plus family and pets. College is set per child (type and share).
- **R5. Resets.** Offer Start over, plus a reset for each section.
- **R6. State assumptions and let people change them.** The chat says what it assumed. Every assumption shown, such as the savings split or yearly saving, can be edited.
- **R7. Changes need confirmation.** No profile change from chat is applied until the person says yes.

## Plan and advice
- **R8. The objective.** "Needs safe. Wants sure. Wishes open." Pay basics in 90 of 100 scenarios, maximise spending you can count on over a lifetime, and show the best way. Decisions are judged at the margin, by running the plan with and without each change.
- **R9. Counted benefits.** Only durable entitlements with clear eligibility count toward the plan. Anything else is shown as information only.
- **R10. Products are information only.** The "Worth checking" items (term life, disability, annuity) carry no price and no recommendation.
- **R11. No made-up numbers.** Every dollar figure in AI text must come from tool output.
- **R12. One figure everywhere.** The same quantity shows the same value in the panel, the plan, the chart and the chat.
- **R13. No dead ends.** Neither chat ever ends with "I can't" and no way forward. Every refusal or failure offers a next step.
- **R14. Refusals.** No advice on hiding assets for Medicaid or SSI, and no single-stock or crypto tips. Emergencies get the 911/988 pointer.

## Look and words
- **R15. Royal Indigo theme (owner, 2026-11-22; tokens in `docs/THEME_ROYAL_INDIGO.css`).** Light by default: indigo canvas, white cards, midnight-indigo headings, one electric-indigo accent. Green for on track, amber for close, rose for short; status colours never for categories. A Deep Space Indigo dark mode (indigo-tinted, never pure black). No pink in use (the palette's 8th chart colour is defined but unused).
- **R16. Layout.** Plan on the left, "Your advisory team" on the right. The team panel draws lines between the lead adviser and each adviser, and shows handoffs on those lines. A What If collapses the plan to a summary bar, with a way back.
- **R17. Ruthless UI.** Show only what drives a decision. Details go behind expanders. Every chart has a hover, tap and keyboard readout of its values.
- **R18. Orwell's plain style.** Short words, active voice, no jargon, no hype. Be plain about uncertainty.
- **R19. Human-like portraits.** Adviser portraits are illustrated people that look hand-made.
- **R20. Accessible and responsive.** Enough contrast, labels, 44 px touch targets, and clear loading and error states, at phone and desktop widths.

## Safety, cost and running
- **R21. Injection-safe.** Code and prompt injection must fail. Text from the browser is treated as untrusted.
- **R22. Secrets.** Never print or commit secrets. The app finds `.env` in its own folder and one level up.
- **R23. Fast and cheap.** The chat answers quickly, and a plan costs a fraction of a cent.
  - `elapsed_s` notes: a plan takes about 8 to 20 seconds on a quiet machine; under load it can take over a minute. The saved eval ran three plans at a time beside pytest: median 11 s, 90th percentile 32.6 s, slowest 91.5 s. The test judges the median (at most 20 s) and the 90th percentile (at most 40 s), not the slowest run.
- **R24. Fresh clone runs in under 10 minutes.**
- **R25. Realistic test inputs.** Tests use realistic, complete households.
