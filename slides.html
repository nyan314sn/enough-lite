How the agents work

AI does the wordsReads what you type (after the rules), writes the plan and Team talk, answers rule questions from 37 cited passages.
Code does the money: it simulates2,000 market paths, plus a bad first decade and high inflation; life tables, plus living to 100 and an early death. About 20 strategies tested: 5 from 467 plans a full optimizer solved ahead of time, plus a rule of thumb and its variants. Not training.
The harnessOwn tools only; at most 4 requests per agent; time limits; caps of $0.05 a plan and $0.50 a session; a number check, one retry, then a template. Every call logged.

MCP server (optional): any AI assistant calls Enough's six tools and gets checked numbers and sources instead of guesses. No AI inside, same engine and refusals, 33 tests. Example: a 58-year-old in Ohio can count on $45,900, $9,100 short of the goal; Medicare cited; Medicaid hiding refused.

One run

A couple, 45 and 43, $310,000 pay, $920,000 saved, three children, two dogs: they can count on $114,800 a year ($44,500 for basics), and the goal lasts in 90 of 100 scenarios. 4. What If tabs on the baseline: retire at 65 gives $140,900; savings $500 more a month, $118,400; markets fall 30%, $108,700 and $6,100 short. Compare puts them side by side.
